Loss can mean a lot of things. I carry a small notebook recently, because it was the only way I felt like I would cope. I jot down small thoughts in it, and I carry it everywhere. It's helped enormously, I'm not sure why. I try to give it more meaning but maybe it's just a little self-centered pleasure in documenting what *you* go through. Seeing that it matters, that the pages accumulate. That something is coming out of it.

Anyway. Loss. I have noticed in this notebook how my pain has changed. The stages I have seen:

1. Muting it. I started by minimizing it. "It was nothing, really".

2. Replacement. Very similar to number 1 but this is more like "I can find something better".

3. Regret. "I could have done better". I should have done this, or that.

4. Mere pain. Lots of crying. Mixed feelings, but overall incomprehensible sadness. Self-depricating feelings. "I wasn't enough".

5. Acceptance. "It was better this way".

Who knows. That's what I wrote while in pain. I still am. So stages will be added. I must say that the toughest part is not number 1, which goes against logic since those are days after, and it's very fresh. The hardest part is 4 and 5. Number 5 is tough. Incomprehensible. The best solution is often times not the most comfortable or even *logic*.
