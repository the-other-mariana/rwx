I've been in love with the idea of nonverbal communication. Maybe because I have been paying more attention to a person with the only purpose of avoiding being disrespectful to him/her. Not because he/she gets offended easily, but because I want to read in between the lines of actions, sentences, questions we presence. I always want to know what do our interactions mean beyond *what we say*.

And I have learned one thing only: *we fucking know*. Nonverbal communication is a whole complete language. And I don't mean the boring theory of, for example, crossing our arms as a sign of confrontation and all those patterns we think we have figured out. No. I mean simply all those facts that *we fucking know* when we talk to someone we're intimite with (and I don't mean sexually, just emotionally even). Does this person holds me dear? We fucking know. Or, if we wonder "does he/she love me?", my God we fucking know. The *way* someone looks at you. We fucking know what that look means. We recognize love in a look from a mile away.

Or when we walk with many people. With who you end up walking with? Do you feel it when it's not your favorite person? Do you feel it when it is? Try walking and talking with someone, and sometimes we don't look at them with the eyes because we are walking and looking to the floor. But sometimes we do. And they know. They *fucking know* when we look. As I continue to like this person more, do I look more into their eyes? Do I ask more questions about their family? Why? What does that mean? As I get to know someone more, do I read more their eyes? I do. And it's very fun to realise *all the things we know*.

The space between text messages. And I don't mean the time lapse each message was sent in, but actually the space of *unsent* messages that surround one message that was sent. Reminds me of Mozart's quote that says that "music is not in the notes, but in the silence between". It's the same. Communication is not in the spoken out loud, but in the unspoken thoughts that surround it. The fact that there's an unsaid means there's something said. So communication is the unsaid in between the said.
